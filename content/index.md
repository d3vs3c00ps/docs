# Ops Notes

Working notes from a systems and platform engineering career. They are written as a
personal reference, not as a course. They are opinionated and sometimes incomplete.

This site grows in batches. A section appears only after I have reviewed it and, where
it can run, tested it.

## Sections

| Section | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| Linux | NixOS: the language, flakes, servers, hardening, automated installs |
| Labs | a NixOS home lab on macOS: Gitea, Woodpecker CI and Kubernetes on VMs |

## A caveat

Notes go stale. Where I know something has changed, a callout says so. Check the
version you run before you paste anything into a production shell.
